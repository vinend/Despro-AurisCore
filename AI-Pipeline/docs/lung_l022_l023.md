# Lung L022 and L023

Research for PRD wheeze, rhonchi and crackle findings. Both experiments use
the L020 HF grouped inner split for selection and the same development
clips for comparison. No application interface changes.

L022 freezes the L020 frame HF checkpoint. On inner clips, use the fixed
0.05–0.95 grid in 0.025 increments. Maximize specificity for wheeze and
crackle subject to recall of 0.81 and the exact L020 development recall,
respectively. For rhonchi maximize F1 at recall of at least 0.75. Break
ties by higher recall then lower threshold. Fall back to the L020 threshold
if the grid has no feasible point. Outer development results are reported
once the thresholds are frozen.

L023 trains from scratch with the L020 backbone, HF fit normalization and
frame weights. Append two shuffled ICBHI cycles to each batch of eight HF
windows. Keep complete cycles of at most five seconds and pool across
complete audio frames. Apply unweighted max-pooled BCE to known cycle labels,
scaled by 0.8 per cycle example. Rhonchi contributes no ICBHI loss. Retain
the HF frame loss, Adam, seed, and early-stopping and threshold rules used in
L020. SPRSound is excluded to isolate the addition of ICBHI.

The ICBHI audit admits only official TRAIN subjects absent from official TEST.
It checks raw audio hashes and normalized PCM fingerprints against the HF
inventory and prior ICBHI records. These checks do not establish cross-source
patient independence or detect all near duplicates. The source manifest
records retained counts and hashes. Official test audio and labels stay sealed.

Run artifacts are under `AI-Pipeline/artifacts/lung-launch/EXP-L022-20261114/`
and `AI-Pipeline/artifacts/lung-launch/EXP-L023-20261114/results/`.
